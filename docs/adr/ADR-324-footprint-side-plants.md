# ADR-324: Plants at the two owner-marked footprint sides

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Accepted.

## Context

The owner marked two additional planting positions in a screenshot: above the
footprint's upper-left portion and beside its right edge below the wall layer.

## Decision

Add one original photographic plant at each marked position, keeping the existing
seven. Reveal these two only near the end of separation, once the moving walls
have cleared their positions. Use the same finite bloom, final fade and reduced-
motion fallback. All other geometry and timings stay unchanged.

## Consequences

The site gains the two requested plants without overlap during layer movement.
