# ADR-332: Final iNDECS wordmark beneath the symbol

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Accepted.

## Context

The owner requested the wording iNDECS below the logo after the visual animation
finishes.

## Decision

Add a left-aligned SVG wordmark below the unchanged symbol, using Google Sans
and the shared heading / navy tokens. After the symbol settles at 4.46 seconds
and movement dust clears, reveal the wordmark at 4.64–4.96 seconds. Extend the
illustration viewport vertically to leave comfortable room beneath the text.

Show the wordmark with the final symbol immediately for reduced motion, skip,
missing images or unavailable JavaScript. Replay hides it until its reveal phase.

## Consequences

The introduction ends in a complete symbol-and-name lockup without changing
existing architectural, plant or logo transition timings.
