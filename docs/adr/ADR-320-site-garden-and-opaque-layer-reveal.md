# ADR-320: Site garden, opaque layer reveal and quicker logo transition

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Accepted.

## Context

The owner requested fewer repeated plants, greenspace and a footpath clear of
all building layers, opaque walls and footprint beneath the roof, and a shorter
transition to the iNDECS mark. This refines ADR-319.

## Decision

Use a small L-shaped garden left of and below the entire swept building envelope,
with lawn, a paved footpath and two plants. Keep a clear gap from the footprint,
walls, outward door and roof throughout their motion. Landscape alone fades in.

Keep lower layers hidden during tile assembly. At the start of separation, switch
their visibility on instantaneously, while they are still covered by the intact
roof. Their photographs are fully opaque, so roof movement exposes existing
material rather than a fade. Retain the final photographic-to-symbol crossfade.

Reduce playback from twelve to eight seconds. Assemble the roof through two
seconds, separate through four seconds, hold briefly, then crossfade to the logo
over roughly half a second. The clean logo is visible by about 6.3 seconds.
Keep replay, skip, Escape and reduced-motion behaviour.

## Consequences

Landscape adds site context without covering the mark or moving layers. The
reveal reads as physical uncovering, and the logo transition feels more immediate.
