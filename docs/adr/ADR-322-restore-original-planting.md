# ADR-322: Restore the original planting

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Accepted.

## Context

The owner requested reversing the palm treatment and returning to the original
repeated planting previously described as too numerous.

## Decision

Restore seven instances of the original photographic plant asset. Remove the
palm and its separate trunk / canopy animation. Position planting left of and
below the building's swept envelope so it cannot overlap moving layers. Retain
the existing finite plant bloom, eight-second sequence, opaque uncovering and
quick logo transition. Keep lawn and footpath absent.

## Consequences

The introduction returns to its original planting style without new image assets.
