# ADR-321: Four plants and a palm unfurl

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Accepted.

## Context

The owner rejected the lawn and footpath treatment in ADR-320 and requested four
plants plus a palm whose trunk fades in before its leaves bloom from the centre.

## Decision

Remove the lawn and paving from the introduction. Position four small plants and
one palm outside the swept building envelope. Fade in the palm trunk first, then
unfurl the photographic canopy using a growing radial reveal and gentle scale.
Natural material colours remain unchanged. Fade all landscape out before the
final mark. Keep the opaque lower-layer reveal and eight-second sequence.

All effects remain finite and respect replay, skip, Escape and both reduced-motion
preferences. The static fallback contains only the unchanged logo.

## Consequences

The landscape consists only of planting, with one distinct palm animation.
