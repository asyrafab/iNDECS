# ADR-330: Earlier logo transition

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: Accepted.

## Context

The owner approved normal-speed overlapping plant blooms and requested the
iNDECS logo animation approximately two to three seconds earlier.

## Decision

Move the photographic-to-logo crossfade from 87–94% to 55–62% of the existing
eight-second timeline: it starts at 4.4 seconds instead of 6.96 seconds and
finishes at 4.96 seconds. Keep the 560 ms crossfade duration. Move the landscape
fade to 52–58% so it clears before the final logo settles. Keep roof separation,
door, tile assembly and the approved random plant timing unchanged.

## Consequences

The logo transition starts 2.56 seconds earlier, after roof separation ends at
four seconds and all plant blooms finish at 3.7 seconds.
